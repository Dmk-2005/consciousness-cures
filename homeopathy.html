The Science & Art

What is Homeopathy?

Homeopathy is a 200-year-old holistic system of medicine founded by Dr. Samuel Hahnemann in 1796. It is based on the principle of "Similia Similibus Curentur" — like cures like — meaning a substance that causes symptoms in a healthy person can cure similar symptoms in a sick one.

Unlike conventional medicine that suppresses symptoms, homeopathy works by stimulating the body's own healing intelligence. Remedies are derived from natural sources — plants, minerals, and animal substances — and are prepared through a process of dilution and potentization that removes toxicity while retaining healing energy.

Is homeopathy scientifically proven?+
Homeopathy has been practiced for over 200 years and is recognized by the WHO as the second largest therapeutic system globally. Multiple clinical studies and meta-analyses have shown efficacy beyond placebo. It is practised by over 500 million people worldwide across 80+ countries.
How long does treatment take?+
Duration varies by condition. Acute conditions may resolve in days to weeks. Chronic conditions like thyroid, PCOS, or skin disorders typically require 3–12 months of consistent treatment. The goal is permanent healing, not just temporary relief.
Can homeopathy be taken alongside other medicines?+
Yes. Homeopathic remedies do not interact with conventional medicines. Many patients use both simultaneously, gradually reducing allopathic medication as healing progresses — always under proper medical supervision.
Is it safe for children and pregnant women?+
Absolutely. Homeopathy is one of the safest systems of medicine and is ideal for children, pregnant women, and nursing mothers. The remedies are gentle, non-toxic, and carry zero risk of addiction or chemical side effects.
