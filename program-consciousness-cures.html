What is Consciousness Cures?

Consciousness Cures is Dr. Suchitra Mylapilli's signature homeopathic consultation practice. It is built on the classical homeopathic principle that each person is unique — and their remedy must be too. Rather than treating disease labels, this program focuses on understanding the complete individual.

Every consultation begins with a 60–90 minute deep case-taking session where Dr. Suchitra explores your physical symptoms, mental state, emotional history, and life experiences. Only after this thorough understanding is a constitutional remedy selected — one that fits you, not just your diagnosis.

The name "Consciousness Cures" reflects Dr. Suchitra's belief that true healing encompasses not just the body, but the awareness and inner state of the person — a principle that connects her Homeopathy practice with her Art of Living journey.
