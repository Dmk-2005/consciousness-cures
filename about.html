A journey that began
at twelve.

Dr. Suchitra Mylapilli's journey began at the age of twelve, when she was in 7th class and attended an Art Excel course — an Art of Living program for children. That experience became her first genuine spark of inner growth. Something shifted in her understanding of herself and the world around her.

As she continued through life, her interest in spirituality grew deeper. When she began her studies in Homeopathy, she found that her inner journey and her medical education spoke to each other in a way that felt natural and complete.

Homeopathy, she discovered, was not merely a system of prescribing remedies. It was a way of understanding the individual — their constitution, their history, their inner state. This resonated deeply with everything she had been exploring through Art of Living.

Today, her life and practice bring these two paths together — Homeopathy and Art of Living — not as separate offerings, but as two expressions of the same perspective on health and well-being.

Prevention is better than cure.

When a person is calm and happy — when they carry a broad, clear perspective on life — she finds that this state of well-being extends naturally beyond the body. It touches relationships, decisions, and the way life itself is experienced.
