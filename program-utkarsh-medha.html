About These Programs

Two programs. One goal: student excellence.

Utkarsh Yoga and Medha Yoga are Art of Living programs specially designed for students at different stages. Both programs combine breathing, meditation, and practical tools that help students perform at their best — academically and personally.

For Ages 8–13

Utkarsh Yoga

Designed for children aged 8–13, Utkarsh Yoga introduces simple, powerful breathing and awareness techniques that help young students stay focused, calm, and confident in school and at home.

  • ✦Improves concentration and memory
  • ✦Reduces stress before exams
  • ✦Builds emotional resilience
For Ages 13–18

Medha Yoga

Medha Yoga is tailored for teenagers aged 13–18 — a more intensive program that addresses the unique pressures of adolescence, board exams, and the transition into young adulthood.

  • ✦Sharpens memory and study ability
  • ✦Manages exam pressure effectively
  • ✦Builds lasting self-confidence
