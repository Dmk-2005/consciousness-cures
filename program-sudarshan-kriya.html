About This Program

What is Sudarshan Kriya?

Sudarshan Kriya is the core breathing practice of the Art of Living Foundation, developed by Sri Sri Ravi Shankar. "Su" means proper, "darshan" means vision, and "kriya" means purifying action — together it means a purifying breath that gives you the right vision of yourself and the world.

The practice uses specific rhythms of breath — slow, medium, and fast — that synchronise with the body's natural biological cycles. This rhythmic breathing releases accumulated stress, toxins, and negative emotions that are otherwise stored at a cellular level in the body.

Participants report better sleep, clearer thinking, reduced anxiety, improved immunity, and a deep sense of calm that persists long after the session. Clinical studies have documented measurable reductions in cortisol (the stress hormone) following regular Sudarshan Kriya practice.
