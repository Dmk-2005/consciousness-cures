About This Program

What is Sanskar Kendra?

Sanskar Kendra is a holistic child development program from the Art of Living Foundation. "Sanskar" means values and good impressions — the foundation we give children to navigate life with clarity, compassion, and confidence.

The program nurtures children through stories, gentle breathing practices, mindful activities, games, and creative expression. It creates a safe and joyful space where children can explore their inner world alongside their outer development.

Dr. Suchitra conducts Sanskar Kendra batches in a warm, caring environment. The program is conducted in-person in weekend batches — please enquire for current batch details and age groups.
