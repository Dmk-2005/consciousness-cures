About This Program

What is Rakheno?

Rakheno is a preventive wellness program rooted in the philosophy that health is best maintained before illness takes hold. The name "Rakheno" means "to keep" or "to maintain" — reflecting the program's core intention: to help you stay well.

Rather than waiting for a health problem to arise, Rakheno works proactively — supporting your body's natural immunity, energy levels, and resilience through the changing demands of daily life and seasonal shifts.

Dr. Suchitra is a certified Rakheno Therapist and offers this program as a complement to her homeopathic practice — for those who want to invest in their health before a problem develops.
