About This Program

What is the Intuition Program?

The Intuition Program is an advanced Art of Living workshop designed to help participants access deeper levels of their own intelligence — beyond the rational, thinking mind. It is one of the Foundation's most unique offerings and is conducted as a residential multi-day workshop.

Through a series of guided processes, meditations, and advanced techniques, participants often experience a heightening of their natural intuitive abilities — greater clarity, sharper perception, and a deepened sense of inner awareness.

Dr. Suchitra is a certified Intuition Teacher and conducts this program in separate batches for children (ages 5–18) and adults — each designed appropriately for the age group. Batches are limited in size to ensure quality.
