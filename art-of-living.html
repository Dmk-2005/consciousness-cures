About Art of Living

The Art of Living Foundation is a non-profit, educational and humanitarian organization founded in 1981 by Sri Sri Ravi Shankar. With a presence in over 180 countries, it is one of the world's largest volunteer-based organizations — committed to eliminating stress and creating a violence-free, disease-free society.

At the heart of the Foundation is the Sudarshan Kriya — a powerful rhythmic breathing technique that harmonizes body, mind, and spirit. Alongside yoga, meditation, and practical wisdom programs, millions have experienced lasting transformation in their mental and physical health.

Dr. Suchitra Mylapilli is a certified Art of Living instructor, bringing these programs to the community — combining the healing of homeopathy with the transformative power of breath and consciousness.
